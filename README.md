## The pitch

> A toothbrush that texts your dentist every time you skip a night, so your six-month check-up opens with a slideshow of your failures.

One sentence, 133 characters. The tiny everyday annoyance is skipping a night's brushing; the ridiculous invention fixes it with shame, delivered as a slideshow at your next check-up.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies, no network) writes the sentence deterministically to `/output/entry.txt`. Run it exactly as the grader does:

```sh
node main.ts
```

Two runs produce byte-identical output.
